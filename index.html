Evolve — a zoo of lasting order

A quality-diversity search (MAP-Elites): every rule is scored (repeating texture × a fractal-dimension band × symmetry, with bonuses for emergent order and ballistic highways) and then filed into a niche by its character — dimension across, symmetry down. Each niche keeps its own champion, so lacy triangles, highways, and crystals all hold seats. Parents are drawn from across the map, which keeps the search discovering different things, not just better ones.

The Law of the Walker

One instruction per cell state; each visit advances the state. In three dimensions the walker carries a full frame — a forward and an up — so the alphabet is N straight · L/R yaw · U/D pitch · C/A roll (roll keeps heading but re-aims what "left" means). State 0 rules all unexplored space, so it must be a turn (L/R/U/D).

Evolve — a zoo of spatial order

The same quality-diversity search as the flat lab, recalibrated for space: repeating texture amplified by a 3-D fractal band (full credit D 1.75–2.65: textured sheets and lacy scaffolds; nothing for threads, helices, or space-filling noise), by 2-D/3-D spread, and by symmetry about the structure's own center (flat axes don't count). Emergent order and ballistic flight earn bonuses. Each niche of the map — dimension across (1→3), symmetry down — keeps its own champion. The evolve arena is the bounded ±5000 cube (the wall ends a run, like the 2-D screen edge); only the live view wraps.
